# World and Lore

**Status:** OPEN — world concept not yet established.

This document will hold the rules and history of the franchise world. Avoid filling it with disconnected ideas before we know what story they serve.

## World design questions

1. What kind of world is this: our world with hidden wonders, an alternate history, or a wholly invented world?
2. What makes travel and exploration difficult, dangerous, and rewarding?
3. How do ordinary people understand legends, ruins, creatures, and supernatural events?
4. What fantastical rules exist, and what are their limits and costs?
5. What civilizations existed before the present day, and what traces remain?
6. How do geography, culture, trade, politics, and belief affect the story?
7. What do different groups want from the world's mysteries?
8. What prevents every mystery from being solved immediately?

## Rule

A location, creature, culture, or magical concept should have consequences and a purpose. Avoid generic fantasy dressing. Distinctiveness comes from the interaction between the world's rules, its inhabitants, and the choices characters make.
