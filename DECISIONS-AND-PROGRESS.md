## Approved creative direction (CANON)

These are project-level creative decisions approved so far. They do not make any specific plot, species, power, or premise canon.

1. The project is an original **sequential, multi-film adventure-fantasy franchise**.
2. Combine **adventure and mystery** with **fantasy and wonder**.
3. The tone and storytelling can include humour, suspense, action, horror, political intrigue, war, emotion, pain, betrayal, friendship, spectacle, and darker moments as appropriate.
4. The films must connect, but each film must also deliver a satisfying central adventure and meaningful resolution.
5. The saga should have a planned underlying truth, foreshadowing, continuity, and payoffs.
6. The universe must be more than human civilizations: include extraordinary lands, different kinds of people and beings, varied powers, and creatures such as dragons, dinosaur-like giants, colossal serpents, guardians, predators, and wholly original species.
7. Creatures must have varied roles and motives: some may help, some may hunt, some may protect territory or safeguard something from misuse, and some may be dangerous without being secretly benevolent.
8. The world should feel alive beyond the protagonist; powers and creatures should generate story, ecology, danger, wonder, and relationships—not just serve as combat tools.
9. Each film should have a distinctive central adventure and satisfying resolution while deepening the larger saga. The goal is to make audiences want to stay in the universe and eagerly anticipate what happens next.
10. The protagonist will be created from scratch, but **hero design is deferred** until the franchise premise and story are clearer.
11. Do not create images unless explicitly requested.
12. Nothing becomes canon until explicitly approved. Preserve rejected ideas so they are not accidentally recycled.
