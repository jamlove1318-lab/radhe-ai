## Approved direction (CANON)

These are project-level creative decisions approved so far:

1. The project is an original **sequential, multi-film adventure-fantasy franchise**.
2. Combine **adventure and mystery** with **fantasy and wonder**.
3. The tone can combine humor, wonder, suspense, danger, emotion, epic spectacle, and darker moments as appropriate.
4. The films must connect, but each film must also deliver a satisfying central adventure and meaningful resolution.
5. The saga should have a planned underlying truth, foreshadowing, continuity, and payoffs.
6. The protagonist will be created from scratch, but **hero design is deferred** until the franchise premise and story are clearer.
7. Do not create images unless explicitly requested.
8. Nothing becomes canon until explicitly approved. Preserve rejected ideas so they are not accidentally recycled.

## Not decided (OPEN QUESTIONS)

- Franchise title.

- Number of films and the plot of each film.
- Protagonist, supporting characters, names, appearances, motivations, and abilities.
- Fantasy rules, civilizations, creatures, locations, and historical events.
- Final conflict and ending.
