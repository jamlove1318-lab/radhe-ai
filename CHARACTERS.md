## Principles

- Supporting characters have lives and goals beyond helping the protagonist.
- Relationships change through events and choices.
- Humor should arise from character and situation.
- Competence has limits; mistakes have consequences.
- Secrets should have a reason to exist and a payoff when revealed.
- Do not copy recognizable character templates from existing franchises.
