- Multiple sequential movies with cumulative continuity.
- A blend of adventure, mystery, and fantasy.

- Every film contributes to an overarching mystery or saga arc.
- Reveal information progressively; do not withhold every answer until the final film.
- Returning characters and earlier discoveries matter naturally.
- The number of films is determined by the story, not a predetermined quota.
