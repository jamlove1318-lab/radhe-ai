# Adventure-Fantasy Movie Universe

**Project status:** Foundation / story concept development  
**Canon policy:** Nothing becomes canon until explicitly approved.

This repository is the source of truth for developing an original, sequential adventure-fantasy film franchise. The goal is a distinctive world and connected saga—not a one-off film and not a copy of an existing franchise.

## Current direction

- Combine **adventure and mystery** with **fantasy and wonder**.
- The franchise will consist of sequential, connected movies.
- Each film should deliver a satisfying adventure, meaningful climax, and some answers of its own while advancing the larger saga.
- The tone can combine humor, wonder, suspense, danger, emotional depth, epic spectacle, and darker moments when the story calls for them.
- Plan the underlying world history and major mystery carefully, with clues and payoffs across films.
- The protagonist has **not** been chosen. Develop the movie premise and franchise first.
- Do not create images or lock character designs until requested.
- Brainstorms are not established facts.

## Canon labels

- **CANON** — explicitly approved.
- **PROPOSED** — under consideration; not established.
- **OPEN QUESTION** — not decided.
- **REJECTED / ARCHIVED** — retained to prevent accidental reuse.

## Session workflow

At the start of each session, read this README and the progress log. Explore options, agree on decisions, then update the relevant files. Before ending, record what changed and the exact next step. Do not silently change approved canon.

## Immediate next step

Develop the **core movie/franchise premise** before choosing the hero, designing characters, making images, or writing full scenes. Find an original central hook, what makes the world worth exploring, and the larger mystery that can sustain multiple movies.
